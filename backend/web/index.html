05 / FROM OBSERVATION TO DECISION

Bring the evidence into your crop plan.

Combine climate history with soil-test values, previous crops, water availability and locally reviewed crop rules. Satellite imagery alone does not choose a crop.

✓
Evidence integrity is built into KISAN-X.Every layer must show its source, date, spatial scale, units and limitations. NASA POWER is a gridded baseline; GPM is a rainfall estimate; MODIS is landscape context; soil nutrients require a verified Soil Health Card or laboratory report. Official IMD forecasts remain separate from historical satellite observations.
